Questions

Before you buy

Sizing, warmth, care, shipping and returns, answered.

The dual-layer weave gives a true sheer look. The outer layer mimics sheer hosiery and the fleece lining, matched to your skin tone, shows through it. In photos and in person they read as regular sheer tights, even up close.

Five sizes: XS/S (90–120 lbs), S/M (110–145 lbs), M/L (135–170 lbs), L/XL (160–200 lbs) and XL/XXL (190–240 lbs). The stretch knit is forgiving, so if you are between sizes either will work. Size up for a more relaxed feel.

The fleece lining is rated for comfort down to about 15°F (−9°C). They are made for everyday winter wear: commuting, shopping, work and nights out. In sub-zero conditions, layer with a longer skirt or dress.

Most fleece-lined tights sag at the crotch after a little wear. Our 3D-knit gusset and graduated compression hold their shape all day, so the silhouette stays smooth from morning to midnight.

Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle inside a mesh laundry bag. No bleach or fabric softener. Tumble dry low or lay flat. The lining is made to stay soft for 50+ washes. Do not iron.

Standard shipping is free and takes 7–12 business days. Express takes 4–7 business days and is free on orders of $50 or more, which includes the Buy 2 Get 1 bundle. Every order ships with tracking and email updates.

A 60-day money-back guarantee. Return unused tights in their original packaging for a full refund. We also exchange for a different size or shade within the same window.

Vanilla suits fair to light skin, Tan light to medium, Bronze medium to olive, and Espresso medium-deep to deep. Choose the shade closest to your natural leg color. When in doubt, go one shade lighter.

The outer layer is knitted with a run-resistant technique and the fleece adds durability, so they hold up far better than ordinary sheer tights. With proper care they will last a full winter and beyond.
